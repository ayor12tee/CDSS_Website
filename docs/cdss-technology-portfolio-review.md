# CDSS technology portfolio review — October 2026

Internal working document. Nothing listed here as a "potential addition" has been published on the site.

**Commercial status key**
- **Verified offering:** CDSS's relationship with the vendor is confirmed (public partner directory or long-standing site content) and the product is current.
- **Potential addition:** relevant to CDSS's customers and sold by a vendor CDSS represents, but CDSS has not confirmed it supplies or supports it.
- **Monitor:** credible but too new, or too narrowly adopted, to feature yet.
- **Retire / rename:** no longer sold, or sold under a new name.

Sources are listed in [cdss-content-research.md](cdss-content-research.md). Accessed 23–24 October 2026.

## Summary of decisions needed from CDSS

1. Which **Ansys** products CDSS licenses (Mechanical, Fluent and so on), and whether the channel agreement continued after Synopsys completed its acquisition in July 2025.
2. Whether CDSS's reseller agreement for the former **Hexagon PPM** (now **Octave**, spun off May 2026) is still in place.
3. Whether to add the potential additions below. The strongest cases are **Leapfrog Energy**, **Autodesk Docs / Construction Cloud** and **ProjectWise** (the last two would back the "common data environment setup" the BIM page already offers), **SACS** visibility, and **HDD PowerTool**.
