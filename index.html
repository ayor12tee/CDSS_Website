About Us

Nigeria's first CADD technology company — still leading, 35+ years on.

Founded in 1989 by Eng. Peter A. O. Fisher, CDSS was Nigeria's first CADD-specific technology company, and went on to install the country's first AutoCAD workstation for Chevron in 1992. More than three decades later, we remain the technology partner behind some of Nigeria's most demanding engineering, industrial and public-sector projects — and were named Bentley Systems' #1 partner in Africa for 2025.

Since 1989

Built by engineers, for engineers.

CDSS was founded by a registered civil engineer who understood, firsthand, what Nigeria's design offices needed: reliable CAD technology, certified training, and support they could count on. That founding discipline still shapes how we work today — every product line we carry comes with vendor-certified training and tiered technical support, not just a licence key.

FAQ

Common questions.

Are the software products you sell genuine and licensed?+

Yes. CDSS is an authorized reseller/partner for every vendor line we carry — Autodesk, Bentley, Seequent, ANSYS, Technical Toolboxes and others — so every license is genuine and backed by the vendor.

How can I purchase software from CDSS?+

Reach out through the contact form below, or by phone or email. We'll scope your workflow and team size, then put together a formal quote.

Do you offer volume licensing for businesses?+

Yes — multi-seat and enterprise licensing is available. Get in touch to discuss terms for your team size.

Do you offer training and certification?+

Yes — certified training across every product line we carry, delivered online or in-person, including our CAD-to-BIM transition program.

What support do I get after purchase?+

Every client is covered by one of our three tiered support plans — from unlimited phone/email support to scheduled onsite visits — plus remote diagnosis via AnyDesk.
