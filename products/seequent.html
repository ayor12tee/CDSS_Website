Seequent

Bringing certainty to the subsurface.

Seequent, part of Bentley Systems, builds the geological and geotechnical modeling software used across mining, civil, and environmental projects — including Leapfrog Geo, MX Deposit, and Oasis montaj. CDSS brings Seequent's subsurface modeling tools to Nigeria's mining, survey, and geoscience sector.

Talk to us about Seequent ← All products

What Seequent software is used for

  • Orebody and resource modeling (Leapfrog Geo)
  • Mine planning and reserve estimation (MX Deposit)
  • Geophysical and geochemical data processing (Oasis montaj)
  • Geotechnical and environmental site modeling

Why go through CDSS

As part of the Bentley family of products, Seequent fits naturally alongside CDSS's existing Bentley relationship and engineering-office client base. CDSS pairs Seequent's subsurface modeling tools with its broader civil, structural, and survey/document-management capabilities — useful for any mining or geoscience operation that needs more than one piece of the puzzle.
