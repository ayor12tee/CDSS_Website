Autodesk

The world's design and make platform.

Autodesk is the company behind AutoCAD, Revit, Civil 3D, and Inventor — software used by millions of architects, engineers, and manufacturers to design buildings, infrastructure, and products. As an authorized Autodesk reseller, CDSS licenses, installs, trains, and supports the full Autodesk AEC and manufacturing line-up for engineering offices across Nigeria.

What Autodesk is used for

  • Architectural design and documentation (Revit, AutoCAD)
  • Civil infrastructure design (Civil 3D, InfraWorks)
  • Structural and steel detailing (Advance Steel, Robot Structural Analysis)
  • Manufacturing and product design (Inventor, Fusion)
  • Construction coordination and clash detection (Navisworks)

Why go through CDSS

CDSS has been an Autodesk reseller and training provider since AutoCAD's earliest days in Nigeria — including the country's first-ever AutoCAD installation, delivered for Chevron in 1992. Every license we sell is genuine and vendor-backed, and comes with certified training and ongoing local support rather than a bare software key.
