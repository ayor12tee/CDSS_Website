Our Values

What has kept clients with us for 35+ years.

We seek to offer our clients the best solutions available in the industry, and to partner with them to grow their business — not just supply a product, but actively train and support the people who use it. Drawing on decades of experience pioneering business solutions tailored to individual clients, CDSS is committed to ensuring you're well supported as you compete in the marketplace.

Integrity & Equity

We adhere strictly to honesty, equity and quality service delivery in every client relationship — the same standard that has kept clients with CDSS for over three decades.

Partnership Over Transactions

We see ourselves as facilitators and consultants to our clients' growth, not just a software vendor — providing the best available solutions, then actively training and supporting clients' use of them.

Ahead-of-the-Curve Technology

From inception, CDSS has sought out technologies ahead of the market's needs, and worked to introduce them — along with the skills to use them — into the Nigerian market.

A Pan-African Footprint

Through partnerships built over 30+ years, CDSS has grown a footprint across East, West and Southern Africa — including Kenya, Zambia, Angola, South Africa, Ghana and, more recently, Côte d'Ivoire.
