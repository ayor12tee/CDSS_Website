About CDSS

35+ years, one engineering technology partner.

CDSS was incorporated in 1989 and became an authorized AutoCAD reseller for Nigeria that same year. More than three decades on, our portfolio has grown to cover nearly everything an engineering office needs — software, hardware, training, technical support, and bureau services — all from one accountable partner.
