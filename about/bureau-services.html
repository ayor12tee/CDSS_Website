Bureau Services

Protect your paper archive before it's too late.

Do you have a large historical archive of paper drawings, from A3 up to A0+? That archive represents the legacy of your organization — and its value shouldn't be underestimated. All of it could be lost to fire, flood, or simple wear. The way to protect it is to scan these documents from paper to digital, and store them in a secure environment where they can later be retrieved, printed, edited, or referenced.

01

Scan

Paper drawings and maps, up to A0+ (44″ wide), unlimited length, in monochrome or color.

02

Convert

Raster-to-vector conversion of scanned drawings and maps where editable CAD files are needed.

03

Store

Securely saved to CD, DVD, or a network storage location (NAS) — or your own external drive.

04

Retrieve

Retrieved, printed, edited or referenced whenever you need it, long after the original paper is gone.

Equipment we use

  • Contex large-format scanners, up to 60″ wide
  • Avision A3/A4 document scanners
  • HP DesignJet large-format plotters and A3 printers

Software behind the service

  • Bentley and Autodesk raster/CAD tools
  • GTX RasterCAD and ImageCAD for raster-to-vector conversion
  • FileHold for document management and archiving

We offer this either as an in-house deployment — supplying and supporting the scanners and software on your own premises — or as a managed bureau service, where CDSS handles the scanning and archiving for you. Contact us for an appraisal of your requirements.
