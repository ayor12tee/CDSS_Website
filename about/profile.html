Company Profile

Nigeria's first CADD technology company.

Incorporated 1989. Still leading, 35+ years on.

CDSS was incorporated in June 1989, and became an authorized AutoCAD reseller for Nigeria in December of that same year. Our work centers on delivering CAD and BIM solutions primarily to the Architecture, Engineering & Construction (AEC) and Oil & Gas sectors — disciplines where CAD/BIM is the standard for producing designs and drawings, from upstream petroleum facilities to buildings and industrial plants. Over more than three decades, that focus has grown into a portfolio covering nearly every engineering-office requirement: software sales, implementation and after-sales support, scanning, printing, plotting, archiving, and training.

What we offer

  • Discipline-specific software for the Upstream Petroleum and AEC industries
  • Large-format scanners — color and monochrome, in 24″, 36″, 42″, 44″ and 60″ widths, unlimited length (Contex)
  • A3/A4 document scanners (Avision)
  • Large-format plotters (HP DesignJet series) and A3 printers

Our services

  • Training, remote or in-person, from our dedicated in-house facility with certified instructors (in person, Zoom or MS Teams)
  • Bureau services — large-format plotting, scanning, archiving and raster-to-vector conversion
  • Engineering services — design and drafting support for building-industry and mapping/GIS clients, on a man-hour basis
  • Technical support, both remote and on-site
  • Customer service through annual support contracts covering installation, commissioning and application support

Backed by 35+ years as an authorized vendor partner

As an authorized vendor partner in Nigeria for a host of internationally recognized software and hardware brands, CDSS occupies a position few others in the market can claim — decades of continuity, backed by qualified and experienced technical and support staff, in a market that keeps evolving.
